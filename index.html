Abuse resistance

The day a payment went missing

  1. Reported

    A Fabilive customer said they had paid, but the money never arrived. People on the team were starting to be suspected.

  2. Investigated

    I followed the payment flow end to end and found the wallet address stored in the system had been changed.

  3. Root cause

    Hidden malicious code in the purchased script was silently replacing our payment wallet with an attacker's.

  4. Fixed

    I removed the backdoor, restored the correct wallet and secured the payment settings. The team was cleared.

Since then, I audit every third-party script and package before it reaches production, and I treat public-facing and payment flows as the most critical parts of any system.
